A positive learning environment

Our rules & expectations

Clear expectations help students, teachers, and guardians work together. These are suggested demo topics, not official policies.

RULE 01

Attendance & Punctuality

Attend classes regularly and arrive on time, ready to learn.

RULE 02

Respect & Discipline

Treat teachers, classmates, and staff with courtesy and respect.

RULE 03

Homework & Practice

Complete assigned work and ask questions when a concept is unclear.

RULE 04

Assessment & Feedback

Take assessments seriously and use feedback to improve.

RULE 05

Communication

Students and guardians should communicate important concerns promptly.

RULE 06

Care for Facilities

Keep classrooms and learning materials clean and in good condition.

RULE 07

Leave & Absence

Inform the institution when a student needs to miss a class.

RULE 08

Fees & Administration

Follow the institution's confirmed fee schedule and administrative guidance.
